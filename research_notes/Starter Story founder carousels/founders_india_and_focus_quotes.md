# Starter Story founders: Indian solo/small-team devs at ~$10K/month, plus founders quoted on deep work and focus

**How this was researched (October 15, 2026).** WebFetch could not reach starterstory.com (DNS error), but `curl` through the session proxy worked. The episode pages, the starterstory.com sitemap and Starter Story's "18 Indian Entrepreneur Success Stories" list were downloaded directly. Every Starter Story episode page (starterstory.com/<name>) carries the video description, a chapter list with timestamps, a "Stack" box and a **full auto-generated transcript**. All quotes below come from those transcripts.

**About the quotes:**
- **"Verbatim (auto-transcript)"** means the words are copied exactly from Starter Story's machine transcript. Only punctuation was added; the transcript has none.
- Product names in the transcripts are often misspelled. Examples: "UForm/Uform" = Youform, "LFS/Eliphos" = Elephas, "Cloud Code" = Claude Code, "Xeno" = Xano, "dig work" = deep work.
- Timestamps are the **chapter start times** listed on the episode page. The quote falls inside that chapter; the exact second was not checked.
- I did not confirm YouTube video IDs for each episode. Cite the starterstory.com episode URL, which links to the YouTube video.

## Which Indian/South Asian solo dev founders on Starter Story fit the ~$10K/month profile?

### Takeaway
Starter Story's own 2025–2026 YouTube episodes describe at least five software founders as "from India" and earning about $10K–$15K/month. Bhanu (SiteGPT, $13K MRR), Louis Pereira (AudioPen, $15K/mo), Vikash (BulkMockup, $12K/mo), Abhishek Chakravarty (Youform, $11K MRR) and Praneeth (Canvas Mode/Rabbitholes, $80K in 6 months, about $13K/mo) are the strongest carousel candidates. Ayush (Elephas, $150K/yr, about $12.5K/mo, two-person team) is a sixth option. The older text-interview list of Indian founders is mostly non-software or larger teams.

## Which Starter Story founders (any country) explicitly talk about deep work, focus or a simple routine as a reason for success?

### Takeaway
Starter Story's 2026 "How I Work" series is the richest source. Marc Lou ($77K/mo) protects a 4–6 hour offline deep-work block every morning. Rob Hallam ($22K/mo, closest to the target band) does an 8-hour uninterrupted night block and says "productivity follows happiness". Jack Friks ($40K/mo) works in sprints with a "one thing per day" rule. Pat Walls, Starter Story's own founder, credits a 2-hour daily deep-work routine inspired by Cal Newport's *Deep Work*. Louis Pereira (India, $15K/mo) is the only Indian founder found who ties success to focus.

### Cited Findings
