## Q1. Tool-by-tool fact base: founders, stage and funding, traction numbers

### Takeaway
The Indian AI dev-tool cohort splits into three tiers:
- **Breakout prosumer app builders:** Emergent and Rocket.new. They reported $4.5M–$100M ARR within months and are the upper bound.
- **Open-source infra and SDK plays that won on GitHub plus the YC/US network:** Mem0, Composio, Supermemory and Keploy. They have 10k–50k+ stars and $3M–$29M raised.
- **Small B2B code-review and testing tools selling to engineering teams:** CodeAnt, Bito, KushoAI, Quash and Potpie. They raised $0.6M–$8.8M and have roughly 10–50+ named customers.

Almost all are US-incorporated or US-HQ, with engineering in Bengaluru or other Indian cities.

### Inferences
- Nearly every successful company here incorporated in the US (Delaware/SF) and kept engineering in India. Emergent had 70 of 75 staff in Bengaluru. "India cost base, US revenue" is the default template.
- Pivots from an earlier Indian dev tool are common and appear to compound: DhiWise became Rocket, Momentum/Waysorted alumni started Potpie, and the PubMatic founders started Bito. Existing user bases and investor relationships carry over.
- Stars-to-revenue conversion varies widely. Keploy has about 18k stars and a small, murky funding history, while Mem0 has about 53k stars and $24M raised. Stars appear to help fundraising most when paired with framework integrations (Mem0 with CrewAI and AWS) rather than on their own.
