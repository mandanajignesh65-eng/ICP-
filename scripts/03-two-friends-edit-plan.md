## Edit tips

- Keep every meme short: 1–1.5 sec. Memes are the punchline, not the story.
- Cut on the voice. Each new line gets a new visual.
- Make Dev 1's part (0:20–0:33) fast and noisy, and Dev 2's part slow and calm. The change in pace tells the story by itself.
- Subtitles: white text with a black outline, 2–5 words at a time, middle of the screen.
- At 83 sec the reel is long. If you want it under 60 sec, cut shots 6, 16 and 17 and speed the voice up to 1.1x.
