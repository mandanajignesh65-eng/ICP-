# Script 3: Same college, 3x salary (faceless reel)

**Format:** Faceless. Voiceover + AI illustrations + screen clips + a couple of memes. ~40 seconds.
**Voice:** Like you're sitting with a friend and telling them about two people you know. Slow, casual, small pauses.

---

## Voiceover

So I have two friends from college. Same batch, same branch, joined almost the same kind of companies. Both use Claude Code every day.

One of them now earns like three times more than the other.

And honestly, I thought he was just smarter. He's not. I sat with both of them for a day, and the difference was kind of funny.

The first one is always busy. He'll start something, then there's a call, then someone pings on Slack, he replies, opens another tab, forgets what he was doing… by evening he's tired, but nothing's really finished.

The other one, before he even opens his laptop, he just writes down what he has to do today. Then he picks one thing and only does that. And every morning he keeps two hours on his calendar where he doesn't take any calls.

I know, I know. This sounds like very basic advice. Write your tasks, do one thing at a time, block some time. Everyone has heard it. But almost nobody actually does it every day. And when you do it daily for months, it adds up. Those two hours are where all his real work happens. The good code, the stuff people actually notice. That's what made the difference, not talent.

He told me he uses this thing called ZeroAI for it. It picks up the Slack requests as tasks and books that focus time on his calendar for him. I've been trying it too. Not bad.
