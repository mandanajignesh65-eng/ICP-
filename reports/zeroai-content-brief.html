Research on dev reels, carousels and posts, plus a verdict on your 4 angles and 10 new ones. Simple version.

Meetings, pings, "quick call?", bugs. Developers share these with teammates.

Know the product

What ZeroAI is, and what it is not

It IS (found in sources)

  • A free Chrome extension: "Zero: You Code. It Handles Slack, Meetings & Calendar."
  • Turns Slack, Jira and Linear asks into tasks
  • Takes meeting notes
  • Books "Deep Work" blocks on Google Calendar
  • Scans your next 2 weeks and shows where focus time went
  • End-of-day close-out that plans tomorrow
  • ~6,000–7,000 users, ~4.9★ rating

NOT found. Ask the client first

  • Replies to your manager for you
  • Writes status updates or the "tracker"
  • Finds the Postman collection
  • Blocks websites
  • VS Code or mobile app

ZeroAI catches the asks. It does not answer them.

RULE 5Your own footage onlySince Apr 2026, repost accounts lose reach.
RULE 6Make it "sendable"Instagram ranks on watch time, likes and DM shares.

Your 4 angles

Verdict: keep #1, fix #2, rebuild #3 and #4

1. Morning chaos POV

KEEP + POLISH

Fix: real Slack/Jira sounds and UI. "Bhai Postman collection bhej de." Add a counter: "Interruption #7 · 11:42". Google an error, not "how to make coding less painful". Chai friend stays friendly. Last 2–3 sec: asks become tasks, focus block booked.

Risk: overacting looks cringe. Don't show ZeroAI finding the Postman file (not confirmed).

As planned8/10
With fixes9/10

2. "3 tools senior engineers use"

FIX

New: "3 boring tools that gave me back focus (#3 is ours)"

Fix: make it a carousel. Drop "senior engineers" unless a real named senior says it. Show each tool doing one concrete thing.

Risk: "this is an ad" comments. Tool lists all look the same.

As planned5/10
With fixes7/10

3. "He gets the hike, you drown in calls"

REBUILD

New: "Same dev. Two Tuesdays." It's not you. It's your calendar.

Fix: split screen. Left: 6 calls, 40 pings. Right: two 2-hour focus blocks. Reward is "logged off at 6:30", not a hike.

Risk: shaming the viewer. The "10x engineer" post (2019) and the 70/90-hour-week comments got huge backlash in India.

As planned3/10
Rebuilt7/10

4. Starter Story founder clip

REBUILD

New: a real Indian indie founder films their own "2 quiet hours" routine (collab post)

Fix: never use Starter Story footage. Use a collab or your own b-roll with voiceover. Talk about the process, not the money. Keep the line "buy quiet time".

Risk: copyright (HubSpot owns Starter Story since Feb 2026). Instagram cuts reach for reposts. Don't invent founder quotes.

With their clips2/10
Own footage7/10
