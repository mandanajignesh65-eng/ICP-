## 1. Can we use it?

**In short:** Facts are free. Photos, logos and video frames are not. Name and face next to an ad need consent.

Why:
- Starter Story terms: re-publish data **only "for non-commercial objectives (e.g. not a company/startup)"**, with a link back ([Starter Story Terms](https://www.starterstory.com/terms)).
- HubSpot bought Starter Story in Feb 2026 ([Adweek](https://www.adweek.com/media/hubspot-media-acquires-starter-story-youtube/)). HubSpot's site terms also say **"non-commercial, personal purposes"** only ([HubSpot Terms](https://legal.hubspot.com/website-terms-of-use)).
- Facts and names are **not** protected by copyright ([Feist v. Rural](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feist_Publications,_Inc._v._Rural_Telephone_Service_Co.); [Copyright Office Circular 33](https://www.copyright.gov/circs/circ33.pdf)).
- Instagram says a credit does **not** fix infringement ([Instagram Help](https://help.Instagram.com/354736791367645)).
- Delhi High Court protected entrepreneur **Ankur Warikoo's** name and face from commercial misuse in 2025 ([WIPO Lex](https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/text/596125)). California law bans using a name or photo in ads without consent ([The Fashion Law](https://www.thefashionlaw.com/resource-center/right-of-publicity/)).
- Since **30 Apr 2026**, Instagram stops recommending accounts that mostly post other people's content, including carousels ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/30/instagram-restricts-reach-of-content-aggregators-in-new-crackdown/)). Own design + own lesson = counts as original.

| Element | Verdict | Safe way |
|---|---|---|
| Photo cut-out (from Starter Story photo or video frame) | **Don't** | Draw our own doodle avatar. Or use a photo the founder sends with written OK. |
| Doodle avatar that looks like the founder | **Risky** | Still their "likeness". Get consent. |
| Founder name | **Risky** (next to a CTA) | OK in story slides. Keep CTA separate. Add "not affiliated" line. Best: consent. |
| Revenue number | **Safe** (fact) | Own words. Add "said on Starter Story, [month year]". Check founder's own X. |
| Tech stack | **Safe** (fact) | Own words. Fix misspelled tool names. |
| Verbatim quote | **Risky** | Paraphrase. Or quote the founder's own X post. Or get their OK. |
| Starter Story logo | **Don't** | No logo. |
| Text credit "Source: Starter Story interview + link" | **Safe** as courtesy | Good manners. It is **not** permission. |
| Video screenshots | **Don't** | Redraw. |
