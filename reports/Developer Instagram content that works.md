# Make Developers Laugh, Then Protect Their Focus

**The answer:** Relaxed developers want to laugh at their own work life: skits about meetings, pings and bugs, deadpan talking heads, and useful carousels they can save. They do not want ads. For ZeroAI: shoot on a phone, edit tight, show one real pain, and show the product only at the end. **"Morning chaos" is strong. "3 tools" needs fixes. "Comparison" and "Starter Story" must be rebuilt** (backlash and copyright risk). Warning: research could not open live pages, so all numbers come from search snippets. Check them before you post.

## 1. What ZeroAI is, and what it is NOT

**In short:** Sell only what the product really does. Some ideas in the brief go beyond it.

> **IS:** A free Chrome extension called "Zero: You Code. It Handles Slack, Meetings & Calendar." ([Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/zero-you-code-it-handles/hplbpdkajdhlggncdpdmnkjldopmoomg))  
> **IS:** It turns Slack, Jira and Linear asks and meetings into tasks, and books Deep Work blocks on Google Calendar. ([ZeroAI](https://zeroai.co.in/))  
> **IS:** It takes meeting notes, runs an end-of-day close-out, and starts with read-only calendar access. It scans the next 2 weeks and "shows where focus time went". ([ZeroAI](https://zeroai.co.in/))  
> **NOT FOUND, so confirm with the client:** No source shows that it replies to managers, writes status updates, finds Postman or API collections, blocks sites, or has a VS Code or mobile app.  
> **Users:** about 6,000–7,000, with a 4.8–4.96 rating. Snapshots differ, so use the number the store shows on the day you post. ([chrome-stats](https://chrome-stats.com/d/hplbpdkajdhlggncdpdmnkjldopmoomg))

ZeroAI **captures** asks. It does not **answer** them.

## 2. Eight rules for developer content

**In short:** Be real, be fast, give value first, and be honest about ads.

| # | Rule | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Lo-fi camera, tight edit.** Shoot on a phone. Use jump cuts, captions and screenshot cutaways. | Ben Awad grew by "just talking deadpan about coding" ([Codecademy](https://www.codecademy.com/resources/blog/ben-awad-tiktok-software-engineer)). Fireship wins with dense, fast cuts ([Engineer's Codex](https://read.engineerscodex.com/p/how-fireship-became-youtubes-favorite)). Developers hate wasted time more than cheap visuals. |
| 2 | **Hook in 1–3 seconds.** | Up to 50% of viewers swipe away in the first 3 seconds ([CapCut](https://www.capcut.com/resource/best-hooks-for-instagram-reels)). |
| 3 | **Reels of 30–60 seconds.** | In 140,000 Reels, 30–60 seconds had the best reach rate (5.60%) ([CreatorFlow/Socialinsider](https://creatorflow.so/blog/instagram-trends-2026-creators-marketers/)). |
| 4 | **Reels for reach, carousels for saves.** | Carousels get 9x more saves than single images ([Metricool](https://metricool.com/instagram-carousels/)). Reels reach more people ([Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-reach-engagement-analysis/)). |
| 5 | **Original footage only. No reposts.** | Since 30 Apr 2026, accounts that mostly repost lose recommendations ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/30/instagram-restricts-reach-of-content-aggregators-in-new-crackdown/)). |
| 6 | **Value or laugh first, product last.** | Mosseri ranks on watch time, likes and **sends** ([CreatorLane](https://creatorlanehq.com/blog/instagram-algorithm-2026-mosseri-vs-measured)). Make posts a dev would DM to a teammate. |
| 7 | **Label your own tool "(ours)".** | Developers distrust self-interested influencers ([Blind](https://www.teamblind.com/post/how-to-crack-xyz-videos-5ra8w1fz)). |
| 8 | **Disclose paid posts (ASCI).** | In India, every paid influencer post needs a clear, upfront ad label ([Nishith Desai](https://nishithdesai.com/research-and-articles/hotline/media-hotline/social-media-vs-reality-check-ascis-new-influencer-guidelines-4713)). |

Also: use licensed business audio only ([OpusClip](https://www.opus.pro/blog/short-form-compliance-rights)).

## 6. On-screen stats

**In short:** Put a small source and year on every stat card. Developers will ask "source?"

**Safe to use (exact wording):**

| Wording on screen | Source |
|---|---|
| "Half of developers lose 10+ hours a week to non-coding work." | [Atlassian DevEx 2025](https://www.atlassian.com/blog/developer/developer-experience-report-2025) |
| "9 in 10 devs lose 6+ hours a week." | [Atlassian DevEx 2025](https://www.atlassian.com/blog/developer/developer-experience-report-2025) |
| "About 6 in 10 devs spend 30+ min a day searching or waiting for answers." | [Stack Overflow 2024](https://survey.stackoverflow.co/2024/professional-developers) |
| "The most-pinged workers are interrupted about every 2 minutes." (Keep "most-pinged".) | [Microsoft WTI 2025 via Axios](https://www.axios.com/2025/06/17/microsoft-remote-work-meetings) |
| "It took about 23 minutes on average to get back to an interrupted task." | [Gloria Mark via Gallup](https://news.gallup.com/businessjournal/23146/too-many-interruptions-work.aspx) |
| "66% of devs spend more time fixing 'almost right' AI code." | [Stack Overflow 2025](https://stackoverflow.blog/2025/07/29/developers-remain-willing-but-reluctant-to-use-ai-the-2025-developer-survey-results-are-here/) |
| "68% didn't have enough uninterrupted focus time." | [Microsoft WTI 2023](https://www.theregister.com/2023/05/10/microsoft_work_trend_index/) |
| "69% of devs lose 8+ hours a week." (**Check the primary report.**) | [DevClass, Atlassian 2024](https://devclass.com/2024/07/15/devs-say-many-of-their-hours-are-wasted-disagree-with-managers-on-how-to-fix-the-issue) |

**Avoid:**
- "It takes 23 minutes to **refocus**." This is the wrong meaning ([Silicon Canals](https://siliconcanals.com/t-work-interruptions-23-minutes-275-pings-organisational-focus/)).
- "Devs code 52 minutes before an interruption", "87 interruptions a day", "devs switch context 23+ times a day". None has a traceable study ([Hatica](https://www.hatica.io/blog/improve-developer-experience/); [Rize](https://rize.io/best/time-tracking-for-developers-2026)).
- "Everyone is interrupted every 2 minutes." It applies only to the most-pinged 20%.
- Competitor claims such as Reclaim's "7.6 hours saved". These are vendor claims.
- "Trusted by 7,000+" if the store shows less that day.

## 7. Hook templates

**In short:** Show the pain in the first frame. Text on screen, sound optional.

1. "POV: you opened VS Code at 10. It's 1 pm. You've written 4 lines."
2. "POV: you said 'quick question' to a senior dev."
3. "What the manager says vs what the developer hears."
4. "Junior dev vs senior dev handling 12 Slack pings."
5. "I counted every interruption today. Guess."
6. "It's not you. It's your calendar."
7. "AI made you faster. Meetings made you slower."
8. "Same dev. Two Tuesdays."
9. "Your calendar after 2 weeks of 'quick calls'." (show the audit)
10. "Stop saying yes to 'quick sync' before lunch."
11. "If standups were a Bollywood movie."
12. "Interview with a developer who has zero meetings."
13. "Send this to the person who books 9 am calls."
14. "3 boring tools that gave me back focus (#3 is ours)."
15. "70-hour week? I'd settle for 2 quiet hours."

Templates, not tested hooks. Test them with **Trial Reels** (shown to non-followers first) ([Manychat](https://manychat.com/blog/trial-reels-the-best-to-test-video-on-instagram/)).

## 8. First six creatives, in order

**In short:** Start with the cheapest high-share reel. Save the founder collab for last.

| Order | Creative | Format | Why this slot |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Morning chaos POV** (fixed) | 30–45 s reel, Trial Reel first | Best angle; tests the voice |
| 2 | **"Where did your week go?"** | 7–10 slide carousel | Shows ZeroAI's real 2-week scan; earns saves |
| 3 | **"Quick call?" deadpan** | 20–30 s talking head | Cheapest to make; built for DMs to teammates |
| 4 | **"Same dev. Two Tuesdays."** | Split-screen reel | Safe version of angle 3 |
| 5 | **"3 boring tools (#3 ours)"** | Carousel | Safe version of angle 2 |
| 6 | **Indie founder "2 quiet hours"** | Collab reel, ad label | Safe version of angle 4; needs outreach time |

After that, repeat the winners with **one recurring face**, the way tl;dv does ([Alt Marketing School](https://altmarketingschool.com/tldv-case-study/)).

## Conclusion

**In short:** ZeroAI's best story is "it's not you, it's your calendar."

No viral reels were found for Reclaim, Motion or Sunsama, so the space is open. ZeroAI can own "the dev's coordination tax". The biggest risk is not bad editing. It is over-claiming features or blaming the viewer. Let the calendar be the villain.
