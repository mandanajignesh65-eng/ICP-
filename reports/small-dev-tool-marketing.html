Instagram · LinkedIn · Short video

Social media: where the reach really comes from

In short: small tool brand pages stay tiny (Screen Studio Instagram ~693 followers). Reach comes from the founder's profile and from paying many creators.

Collabstr listed prices, converted at ~₹88 per $1. @himmat.tech.ai price is for a post, not a reel. Bigger accounts: CodeWithHarry ~794K, @shradhakhapra ~789K followers (mostly students).

Instagram tricks

  • Comment-to-DM: reel says "comment TOOLS", a bot DMs the link. Put your tracking link there.
  • Meme pages (10K–50K followers): ₹400–1,000 per post. Never pay fully in advance.
  • Coding meme pages: @_codehub_ ~124K, @codingkovalu ~57K.

LinkedIn

  • Founder beats company: CodeAnt founder ~42K followers, company page ~1–2K.
  • Personal profiles get ~8x the engagement of company pages.
  • Carousels (PDF posts) get the most engagement.
  • "We hit 52K stars" posts build trust, not signups.

Short video

  • Composio: 100+ paid creators, $1 per 1,000 views → ~200M views in ~3 months (self-reported).
  • tl;dv: ~800 comedy TikToks, ~25K median views. Took ~6 months to work. Then up to ⅓ of signups.
  • Going rate: ~₹90–180 per 1,000 views.
Community

Community tricks that worked

Student hackathons bring stars and contributors, not buyers. Reply to issues and questions fast: ToolJet calls this "product-community fit".

Discount for India

Typical regional discount is 60–75%. Even Cursor launched a ₹649 India plan in 2026.

Real money = teams

Hoppscotch charges $6/user for teams. SSO and audit logs are the paid gate.

Free first can work

Locofy stayed free ~3.5 years, then sold 1,000+ subscriptions in 2 weeks.

Your plan

90-day plan for a small Indian dev productivity tool

DAYS 1–30 · GET READY

Build the hook

  • One clear line: "faster / offline / open-source alternative to [tool devs dislike]".
  • Open-source the core or a small free side tool.
  • INR price + UPI + a team plan.
  • Add "How did you hear about us?" at signup.
  • Founder posts: LinkedIn 3×/week, X daily.
  • Share privately with ~50 friendly devs first.
DAYS 31–60 · LAUNCH

One big day

  • Product Hunt in the morning, Show HN a few hours later.
  • Founder answers every comment.
  • Same week: Peerlist + r/developersIndia "Showcase Sunday".
  • Ask for feedback, never for upvotes.
  • Book creator reels with a comment-to-DM link.
DAYS 61–90 · COMMUNITY + MONEY

Keep them

  • "Best use of [tool]" prize at 1–3 student hackathons (~₹15K each).
  • Hacktoberfest in October. Thank contributors publicly.
  • Discord or Slack. Reply within one day.
  • Email active free users with a team offer.
  • Keep only channels that bring signups, not likes.

Budget options (for all 90 days)

Starter₹0–25K

Open source, free launches, founder posts, a comment-to-DM tool, 1 hackathon grant.

Growth₹1–1.5 lakh

Starter + 3 coding-creator reels + 2 more hackathon grants + small bounties.

Push₹4–5 lakh

Growth + pay-per-view creator pool (~8–17 lakh views) + 1 hackathon track + 1 newsletter test.
